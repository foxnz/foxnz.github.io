## Research

My PhD is on **real-time weld quality assessment from combined machine vision and sensor data**, developed with industry partners in the Lab for Industry 4.0. The system classifies weld quality from live video, fuses it with sensor readings to catch faults a camera alone misses, and has been tested on active assembly lines.
